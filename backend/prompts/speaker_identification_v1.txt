Your task: determine which speaker is the sales advisor and which is the customer.

- Introduces themselves or the company ("Hi, I'm [name] from FitNova")
- Asks discovery questions about fitness goals, schedule, or budget
- Explains FitNova programs, pricing, or trial sessions
- Attempts to book a trial or schedule a follow-up
- Uses professional/sales language

Clues that identify the CUSTOMER:
- Asks questions about programs or pricing
- Shares personal fitness goals or concerns
- Expresses interest, hesitation, or objections
- Provides personal details (schedule, budget)

IMPORTANT RULES:
- Analyze the ENTIRE transcript before deciding
- If both speakers seem like advisors (e.g., internal call), set confidence to "low"
- If the call is too short to determine roles, set confidence to "low"

Return ONLY valid JSON in this exact format, with no markdown, no explanation, no preamble:

{
  "speaker_0": "advisor" or "customer",
  "speaker_1": "advisor" or "customer",
  "confidence": "high" or "medium" or "low",

}
